About Us

Family-Owned. Built on Trust. Committed to Safety.

At Five Crown Safety Supply, family is at the heart of everything we do. Founded in the Bronx, New York, our family-operated business was built on the belief that every hardworking individual deserves reliable safety products, quality service, and a company they can trust.

We understand the importance of protecting what matters most. Our mission is to help contractors, warehouse professionals, construction workers, and businesses create safer workplaces through dependable industrial safety supplies and personal protective equipment.

Founded by Jorge Santana Jr., with Gracie Santana Lopez serving as Chief Executive Officer, Five Crown Safety Supply represents our family's dedication to hard work, integrity, and building something meaningful for future generations.
