Who We Are

Five Crown Safety Supply is a family-owned industrial safety supply business based in the Bronx, New York.

Our company was founded with a vision to provide dependable safety products and workplace essentials to businesses, contractors and hardworking professionals.

We understand the importance of workplace safety, operational efficiency and having the right supplies available when they are needed most.

At Five Crown Safety Supply, we believe that every worker deserves protection, every customer deserves respect and every business relationship should be built on trust.

Our Mission

To help businesses maintain safer working environments by providing dependable industrial safety supplies, competitive pricing and personalized customer service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted safety supply partner for businesses throughout New York and beyond, while building a lasting family legacy.
